## Shot list and prompts

Each shot is made in two steps: a still keyframe (to lock the look), then a 5-second video from that keyframe. Shots marked **REAL PRODUCT** use your own product photos.

**Style line added to every prompt:** *cinematic luxury fashion-house campaign, 35mm film look, shallow depth of field, deep blacks, warm gold light, dark walnut and winter ivory palette, slow deliberate camera, realistic human movement, no text, no logos.*
