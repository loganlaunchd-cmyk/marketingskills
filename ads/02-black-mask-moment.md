## Visual brief

**Idea:** The sensory moment of opening the jar and applying the peat mask.

**Shots (10-12 s):**
1. 0-2 s: Top-down, the black jar lid twists off on travertine stone. On-screen hook.
2. 2-5 s: Macro of a fingertip scooping the mask; show the real texture as it is.
3. 5-8 s: Brush or fingers spreading an even layer on a cheek or jawline (crop: lower face only, no eyes needed).
4. 8-10 s: Rinsing with warm water over a stone basin, slow motion.
5. 10-12 s: Jar beside The Balance, logo end card.

**Setting and look:** Bathroom with stone and linen, warm side light, cream towel, olive branch. Calm, slow cuts.

**Formats:**
- **9:16:** the texture macro fills the frame; keep text in the centre band.
- **1:1:** a still of the open jar with the texture visible, or shots 2 and 3 as a 6 s loop.

**Avoid:** Before/after skin shots, "after" skin close-ups that imply a result, zoomed pores. Turn the jar so the "100% Natural" badge and "Detoxifying" line are not legible.

**Check before filming:** Confirm the mask's real colour and texture on camera. The headline concept says "black"; show what the product actually looks like.
