## Visual brief

**Idea:** The small daily pleasure of a good bar: lather, scent, cool water.

**Shots (10 s):**
1. 0-2 s: The black box slides open; the dark bar is lifted out. On-screen hook.
2. 2-5 s: Macro of the bar turning under running water, rich lather forming between palms.
3. 5-7 s: Sprigs of rosemary, peppermint leaves and lemongrass beside the bar on wet stone.
4. 7-10 s: Cool water splashed on the face (eyes closed, calm), towel pat. End card.

**Setting and look:** Morning light, stone washbasin, linen, fresh herbs. ASMR-style sound of water and lather works well without music.

**Formats:**
- **9:16:** the lather macro is the hero shot; captions in the safe band.
- **1:1:** still of the bar on wet travertine with herbs, the box slightly behind.

**Avoid:** The box text "Antibacterial Lemongrass" and the "Handmade & Natural" badge must not be legible. Show the bar or the plain side of the box. No skin-result close-ups.
