**Platform:** Meta (Facebook and Instagram), feed, Stories and Reels  
**Product(s):** Any product or ritual the real review is about  
**Grounding:** Must be grounded in a real customer review the owner supplies, used with that customer's written permission  
**Audience stage:** Warm / retargeting (engaged with earlier ads or visited eluora.co).  
**Landing page:** eluora.co [TO CONFIRM exact product or ritual URL]

**Test hypothesis:** Once a real review is in place, beats concept 01 on purchase conversion rate in the warm audience. Do not run it until a real review exists.

Character limits checked: hook up to 60 (on-screen text and first line of primary text), headline up to 40, first 125 characters of primary text shown before "See more".

## Visual brief

**Idea:** The customer's own words on screen, with the product, and nothing invented around them.

**Shots (8-10 s):**
1. 0-4 s: Cream background, the review text appearing line by line in the ELUORA serif (quote marks in gold).
2. 4-7 s: The reviewed product on travertine, gentle push-in.
3. 7-10 s: Product name, price, logo.

**Formats:**
- **9:16:** quote centred in the safe band, maximum 3 short lines on screen.
- **1:1:** a single static quote card with the product photo in the lower third.

**Rules for using this template:**
- Paste the review **verbatim**. Do not edit, shorten in a way that changes meaning, or combine reviews.
- Use only a real review you can trace to a real customer. Keep a record of the source and their written permission.
- No star ratings, no "5/5", no "verified" badges unless they come from a real review platform you can show.
- If the review mentions a skin or scalp condition (for example acne or dandruff) or a dramatic result, do not use it.
- If the review contains a banned word (natural, organic, detox, and so on), choose a different review.
- First name only, and only with permission. No photos of the reviewer unless they agreed in writing to appear in ads.
