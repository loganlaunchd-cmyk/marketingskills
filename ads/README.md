## How to read the copy

- **Hook:** the first line of the primary text, and the on-screen text for the first 2-3 seconds of video. Up to 60 characters.
- **Headline:** shown under the image or video. Up to 40 characters.
- **Primary text:** shown above the creative. Only the first 125 characters show before "See more", so the hook is always first.
- **CTA button:** from Meta's standard button list (Shop Now, Learn More).
- **Product names:** the catalogue name leads and the shop name sits in brackets the first time, e.g. "The Cleanse (Bog Bar)". Headlines use the catalogue name only, to fit 40 characters.
- **Scalp bundle:** called "The Scalp Reset Ritual" in ads (the shop name contains a banned word).

## Rules applied to every file

- No before/after images and no invented results on skin or hair.
- No reviews, testimonials, star ratings or customer names written by us. Concept 08 is a template with a marked placeholder for a **real** review.
- No medical claims (acne, eczema, psoriasis, dandruff, hair loss, hair growth).
- None of these words: natural, organic, eco-friendly, sustainable, detox, detoxifying, antibacterial, clinically proven.
- Cosmetic wording only. No discount language, no exclamation marks, no em dashes.
- The catalogue's results lines ("results from the first use", "visibly revitalised after every use", "strengthens", "restores pH") are **not** used.

## Production notes for all video

- **Packaging:** several labels show banned words ("Detoxifying" on The Purify and in The Reset ritual photo, "Antibacterial Lemongrass" and "Handmade & Natural" on The Cleanse box, "100% Natural" on The Purify jar). Frame shots so that this text is not legible.
- **9:16 (1080x1920):** keep text and key visuals between y=220 and y=1420, with 180 px side margins (Reels and Stories UI cover the rest).
- **1:1 (1080x1080):** feed placement; use the hero still or a short loop.
- **Length:** 8-15 seconds. Hook on screen within the first 2 seconds. Captions on, because most people watch without sound.
- **Look:** cream, warm beige, travertine stone, linen, moss, olive leaves, soft low sun, gold ELUORA logo (as in the catalogue photography).
- **No image or video tools have been used.** If you want me to generate visuals with a tool (for example Higgsfield), I will first send you the plan and the credit or € cost for approval.
