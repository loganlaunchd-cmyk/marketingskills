## Visual brief

**Idea:** A slow journey from the bog at dawn to the bottles on stone.

**Shots (15 s):**
1. 0-3 s: Wide, misty Estonian bog at sunrise, still water reflecting pines (matches catalogue cover). On-screen hook.
2. 3-6 s: Close-up of moss and dark peat, a hand touching the surface.
3. 6-9 s: Slow push-in on the six products arranged on pale travertine stone with soft leaf shadows (catalogue p.3 style).
4. 9-12 s: Text card: "elu = life" in the ELUORA serif, cream background.
5. 12-15 s: Logo end card with eluora.co.

**Setting and look:** Real Estonian bog footage (own or licensed stock), cream and warm-beige studio palette, gold logo, soft daylight from a window, no people's faces needed.

**Formats:**
- **9:16 (1080x1920):** keep all text inside the centre band (y 220 to 1420, 180 px side margins).
- **1:1 (1080x1080):** use shots 1, 3 and 5 only (about 10 s), or a single still of the bottles on stone with the bog softly behind.

**Avoid:** Legible close-ups of labels that say "Detoxifying", "Antibacterial", "Natural" or "100% Natural". No before/after.
