## Visual brief

**Idea:** Each ingredient shown as a real object, then the bottle it goes into.

**Shots (15 s), one ingredient per 2-3 s with a single-word label:**
1. "Peat": a hand holding dark peat, moss around it.
2. "Peat water": amber water poured slowly into a stone bowl.
3. "Argan": argan oil dripping from a glass dropper.
4. "Olive": an olive branch laid on linen, oil in a small dish.
5. "Aloe vera": a fresh aloe leaf cut open, clear gel.
6. End: the six products in a row, "What's inside ELUORA", logo.

**Setting and look:** Top-down on cream travertine, shadows of leaves, warm light. For variations 2 and 3, end on The Restore or The Daily only.

**Formats:**
- **9:16:** one ingredient per screen; words centred in the safe band.
- **1:1:** 6-card carousel (5 ingredients + product card) or a grid still of all five ingredients around one bottle.

**Avoid:** Implying these are the only ingredients ("made with just 5 ingredients"). Avoid words like "pure" or "natural" in on-screen text.
