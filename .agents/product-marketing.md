## Compliance Guardrails (read first — applies to every skill)

These rules override anything else in this document, including wording taken from the catalogue.

- **No before/after images** and **no invented results** on skin or hair.
- **No fake reviews, testimonials, star ratings or customer names.** Reviews only from real, consented customers, quoted verbatim.
- **No medical claims.** Never mention acne, eczema, psoriasis, dandruff, hair loss or hair growth (not even "not for…").
- **Banned words:** natural, organic, eco-friendly, sustainable, detox, detoxifying, antibacterial, clinically proven.
- **Cosmetic wording only:** cleanses, refreshes, softens, hydrates, "appearance of pores", "healthier-looking hair".
- **No discount-style language:** no "SALE", "% off", urgency countdowns, "limited time", exclamation-heavy copy.
- **No image, video or paid generation tools and no ad spend** without showing the plan and cost and getting approval.

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Scalp, hair and face care made with Estonian peat and peat water.

**What it does:** ELUORA "brings peat and peat water into modern scalp and facial care through thoughtfully developed formulations" (p.2). It is a six-product retail collection that "pairs cleansing and conditioning for the scalp with a complementary range for the face" (p.3), sold individually and as three at-home rituals.

**Name origin:** "ELUORA begins with *elu*, the Estonian word for life. Inspired by the landscapes of Estonia…" (p.2)

**Product category (shelf):** Premium scalp & hair care and facial care; peat-based Nordic beauty.

**Product type:** Consumer e-commerce (eluora.co) plus retail partners — "We work with a small, intentional circle of retail partners and voices who care about where their beauty products actually come from." (p.14)

**Channels:** Website www.eluora.co · Instagram @eluora.official

**Business model and pricing:** Direct-to-consumer on eluora.co, prices in EUR (owner-supplied price list, 2026-11-16). Shipping about €5 to most EU countries; **free shipping on orders over €80** (so all three main rituals ship free, single products do not). Ships to: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden (owner, 2026-11-18). **Last order date for Christmas delivery: 19 December.**

## Differentiation

Based only on the catalogue:
- **Peat and peat water across scalp, hair and face** — one ingredient story across a full six-product routine.
- **Ritual structure** — clear daily/weekly routines that pair products (p.11–13).
- **Estonian origin and name** — *elu*, the Estonian word for life.
- **Gentle positioning** — "without stripping moisture" (The Reset), "without stripping the skin's moisture barrier" (The Cleanse), alcohol-free toner.
